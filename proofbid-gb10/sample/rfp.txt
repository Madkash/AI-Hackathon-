Healthcare Network — Supplier Technology RFP
1. The solution must support PostgreSQL 14 or later.
2. Vendor must provide evidence of SOC 2 Type II.
3. Audit logs must be retained for 365 days.
4. The solution must support Oracle database.
5. The solution must enforce TLS 1.2 or greater.
6. Administrators must use MFA.
7. The solution must support RBAC.
8. Specify minimum RAM requirements.
